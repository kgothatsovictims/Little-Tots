Admissions at Little Tots

We make the enrollment process simple and stress-free for parents. Below is everything you need to know to get your child started with us.

📞 Call to Enroll Your Child

Required Documents for Admission

To ensure a smooth enrollment process, parents are kindly requested to provide the following documents:

  • Child’s Birth Certificate
  • Clinic Card / Immunisation Record
  • Parent or Guardian ID Copy
  • Proof of Address
  • Completed Enrollment Form
Speak to Us About Enrollment

Book a Visit

We encourage parents to visit Little Tots, meet our staff, and see our facilities before enrolling their child.

Book a visit today and let us show you why Little Tots is a safe, nurturing, and joyful place for your child.

We Also Offer Transport Services 🚌

For your convenience, Little Tots offers a safe and reliable transport service for children living in surrounding areas.

Our transport service is supervised, child-friendly, and carefully managed to ensure your child arrives safely and comfortably each day. Availability is limited.

  • Safe & reliable transport
  • Trained drivers & assistants
  • Morning pick-up & afternoon drop-off
  • Limited spaces available
